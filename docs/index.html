Politecnico di Milano

Paper PDF Additional Material

Overview

FRA-NBV is a reflectivity-aware next-best-view strategy for active 3D reconstruction in robotic perception. The method targets challenging reflective scenes by explicitly considering both reconstruction coverage and sensing reliability.
