Concave Objects Qualitative Results

Concave objects and mutual reflections are challenging because changing the angle of incidence may not always be sufficient to recover missing depth measurements. We performed an additional qualitative evaluation on four objects characterized by reflective surfaces, concave regions, or both. CAD models were not available for these objects, so the comparison is qualitative. Overall, FRA-NBV remains less affected by concave and reflective regions than the PB-NBV baseline.
