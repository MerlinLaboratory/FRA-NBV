Additional qualitative material

Concave and Reflective Objects

Concave objects and mutual reflections are challenging because changing the angle of incidence may not always be sufficient to recover missing depth measurements. We therefore performed an additional qualitative evaluation on four objects characterized by reflective surfaces, concave regions, or both.

CAD models were not available for these objects, so the comparison is qualitative. The reconstructions obtained with PB-NBV and FRA-NBV are shown below. Overall, FRA-NBV remains less affected by concave and reflective regions than the PB-NBV baseline.

Object 1

Reflective Appearance

Visual appearance alone was not sufficient to predict the difficulty for the depth sensor. For Object 1, PB-NBV and FRA-NBV remain largely comparable, with FRA-NBV yielding a slightly more compact reconstruction.

Object 2

Chrome Finish

Object 2 appeared highly reflective due to its chrome finish. The reconstruction results show only minor differences between PB-NBV and FRA-NBV, again with FRA-NBV producing a more compact reconstruction.

Object 3

Concave Reflective Configuration

Object 3 was built from multiple instances of the most reflective item used in the quantitative experiments, arranged to form a concave region. PB-NBV struggles more in the inner part of the concavity, whereas FRA-NBV provides a more complete reconstruction.

Strong Reflectivity and Concavity

Object 4 combined strong reflectivity with a concave region. One face inside the concavity was difficult to acquire, and FRA-NBV produced a visibly more complete reconstruction than PB-NBV.
